# Eden demo

A fictional call of about two minutes. Use **Luna low** and an **Eden (demo)**
contact with this approved memory:

> Eden recently lost a part-time job at a bookshop and is looking for work.

The spoken script never names the job or employer. Financial pressure gives the
app a chance to recall the memory indirectly. The local memory model must be
installed for semantic retrieval; see [Setup](README.md#setup).

## Recording script

Speak naturally and leave room for replies. This version was paced at about
110 seconds, with the first six lines taking roughly 35–38 seconds.

**Simon:** Hi Eden, good to see you. Would you like to catch up tomorrow?

**Eden:** I'd like that. I've been a little stressed lately, to be honest.

**Simon:** I'm sorry to hear that. What's been on your mind?

**Eden:** Money's been really tight. I'm watching every expense, so I need to keep things simple.

**Simon:** We don't have to do anything expensive. What would make it easier?

**Eden:** Even a meal out adds up right now. I still want to see you; I just can't spend much.

*Show Related memories and open the reminder's Sources. Keep the employment
details out of the spoken conversation.*

**Simon:** How about a walk instead? We could bring something from home.

**Eden:** That sounds better. I'd rather not talk about the numbers today, though. I just want to relax.

**Simon:** Of course, we can leave that alone. Would the park work?

**Eden:** Yes. I prefer somewhere quiet. In crowded cafes I find it hard to follow the conversation.

**Simon:** Then how about the park entrance tomorrow at five PM?

**Eden:** Five PM works for me. Let's keep it to half an hour, if that's okay.

**Simon:** Half an hour is fine. I'll bring water. Would you like some?

**Eden:** Yes please. Thanks for being understanding. I'm actually looking forward to it now.

**Simon:** Great. I'll see you at the park entrance tomorrow at five PM.

**Eden:** Perfect. A quiet walk sounds lovely. See you tomorrow!

## Extra opening for faster speech

If the first six lines take about 25 seconds, insert these lines immediately
after them, before the walk suggestion. The whole extended script is 301 words,
about 93 seconds at that pace; its financial opening lasts about 54 seconds.

**Simon:** We can make this easy. Is it the little everyday costs that are adding up?

**Eden:** Exactly. I keep checking my balance before I buy anything, even something small.

**Simon:** That sounds tiring. You don't have to spend money just to hang out with me.

**Eden:** Thanks. I don't want to cancel everything. I just need to be careful for a while.

**Simon:** Then let's choose something free, with no tickets, no meal to pay for, and no pressure.

**Eden:** That would be a relief. I mostly want some company and a chance to get out of the house.

## Video sequence

1. Show the selected contact and saved memory, then start the call.
2. Follow either script. Show tones, cues, responses and the recalled memory.
   Existing suggestions stay through the boundary lines; older items can be
   marked **Earlier**. New items fill four slots and retained items expire after
   180 seconds. Scroll or open a pop-out to read longer suggestions.
3. End the call, show its summary and memory proposals, correct any unsupported
   wording, then approve the items you want to keep.
4. In Contact information, ask **“What did we agree to do tomorrow, and when?”**
   Show the answer and its sources. The recording script explicitly says five PM
   so the model does not need to guess AM or PM.

Actual API wording and timing vary. This is a staged recording; review suggestions
before using them or saving them as contact information.
